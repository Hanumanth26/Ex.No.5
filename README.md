# 1. Introduction

Prompting is a foundational technique in AI language models, where the phrasing of inputs (prompts) determines the relevance and quality of the outputs.
Different prompting patterns—such as zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, role-based, and contrastive—serve various purposes depending on the complexity and context of the task.
In this experiment, we analyze these patterns within the context of a Smart Health Assistant System by applying them to test scenarios such as symptom prediction, lifestyle suggestions, and health risk assessment.
